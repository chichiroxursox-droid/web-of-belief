# Occurrent and dispositional beliefs are real but distinct phenomena

**Claim:** Most of what I 'believe' is not actively entertained — it is a standing disposition (that Paris is in France, that 2+2=4). An occurrent belief is the rarer subset currently at the fore of attention. Both are beliefs; the dispositional ones are not lesser for being latent.

**What it would take to refute this:** Evidence that the subjects typically called 'dispositional believers' (e.g., everyone asleep right now) fail any serious test of believing the thing in question upon waking — i.e., that the label 'belief' does no explanatory work for latent states.
