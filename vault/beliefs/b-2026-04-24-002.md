# Dispositions are identified holistically, not atomically

**Claim:** A single action under-determines the disposition behind it; we identify dispositions by the pattern across many actions and contexts.

**What it would take to refute this:** A demonstration that any single observable act uniquely identifies the underlying disposition, independently of other observations.
