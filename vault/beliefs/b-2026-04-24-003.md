# Belief is a propositional attitude toward a content P

**Claim:** To believe is to take an attitude — specifically the 'taking-as-true' attitude — toward some proposition P. This distinguishes belief from hoping, fearing, doubting, or intending, each of which takes different attitudes toward the same P.

**What it would take to refute this:** A demonstration that there exist genuine beliefs not reducible to any attitude-toward-content structure — e.g., a coherent account of belief that treats it as purely a behavior pattern with no internal 'taking' at all, accepted by most working philosophers of mind.
