# A belief is a disposition to act as if P

**Claim:** To believe that P is to be disposed to act, assert, and infer as if P were true, across a range of contexts.

**What it would take to refute this:** Evidence of a subject who sincerely asserts P but whose actions systematically assume not-P across unrelated contexts, with no available explanation (self-deception, weakness of will, etc.), would undermine the dispositional account.
