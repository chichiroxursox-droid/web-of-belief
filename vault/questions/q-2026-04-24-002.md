# Can beliefs exist without the capacity for language?

**Question:** The SEP entry flags this as a distinct open question in philosophy of mind (§4). Worth pursuing because it bears on whether belief is essentially tied to propositional content or whether something more primitive (perceptual, pre-linguistic) counts.

**Why it matters:** Source: SEP Belief §4. Possible entry points: Davidson 1984, Bermudez 2003.
