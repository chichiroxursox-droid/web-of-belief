# Dickinson, '"Hope" is the thing with feathers—' (c.1861, Johnson #254)

**Summary:** A short lyric. Hope figured as a small bird that perches in the soul, sings without words, sings sweetest in storms, asks nothing in extremity. Three quatrains, ballad meter, dashes pacing the lines. Read directly (not via critical commentary) for the aesthetic-experiment: pick a poem and attend to whether anything happens on reading.
