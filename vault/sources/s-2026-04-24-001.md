# SEP entry: Belief (Schwitzgebel)

**Summary:** Overview of contemporary analytic-philosophy theories of belief. Characterizes belief as the paradigmatic 'propositional attitude' — the mental state expressible as 'S believes that P'. Surveys five main accounts: representationalism (beliefs are discrete mental representations), dispositionalism (belief = disposition to act/assert/infer as if P), interpretationism (belief-ascription is whatever best explains observed behavior), functionalism (belief is defined by causal role), and eliminativism (folk-psychological belief-talk doesn't carve nature at its joints). Notes that most accounts are pluralist in practice. Distinguishes occurrent vs dispositional belief, degrees of belief (credence), and raises whether non-linguistic creatures can believe.
